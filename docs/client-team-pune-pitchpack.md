## 4) CLOSER — replies to the 6 common responses

Every reply: acknowledge → reframe to the business cost → one micro-proof → ask for 15 minutes with two time options.

**"How much?"**
> Fair question. Before I give a number that means something, it depends on one thing: one page or a full site with booking. For most of what I saw on your page, we're talking about a small fixed project — and I'll give you the exact figure on a 15-minute call, no padding. Are you free tomorrow around 11, or after 6?

**"Send your portfolio"**
> Sure — [link]. Two things worth knowing: I keep it short, and the first thing I'd do for you is the same 30-second fix I mentioned, before any money. Can I walk you through the two closest examples in 15 minutes? Today evening or tomorrow morning works for me.

**"Not right now"**
> Understood — I won't chase you. One thing though: the issue I mentioned is live right now, and it's the kind that quietly costs bookings every week it stays. Should I check back in a month, or is there a better time — say after [festival/month]?

**"We already have someone"**
> Good — that's usually better than starting fresh. I'm not asking you to replace anyone. Let me do one free check of the two things I flagged and send you the notes; you forward them to your developer. If they fix it, you've lost nothing. Worth 15 minutes to see what I found?

**"How long will it take?"**
> The small piece — the booking/menu/contact fix — is a couple of days once I have access. A full one-page site is about five working days from the day I get your photos and details. The slow part is never the build, it's collecting content. Shall we do 15 minutes so I can tell you exactly what I'd need from you?

**"Interested, tell me more"**
> Great. Three things I'd do in order: fix the broken thing I flagged, put one page up that takes orders or bookings, then connect enquiries straight to your WhatsApp so nothing sits unanswered. I'd rather show you than type it. 15 minutes tomorrow at 11, or the day after at 5?

### Follow-up plan (no reply = no pressure, still specific)

**Day 3 — one line, same channel, no guilt trip:**
> Hi [Name], quick one — did the [specific issue] message reach you? Happy to send the fix notes either way, even if you don't want to work together. Just say yes and I'll drop them here.

**Day 7 — close the loop, give value, make it easy to say no:**
> Hi [Name], last message from me on this. I checked again today: [issue] is still there. Two options — (a) I send you the 3-step fix to hand to anyone, free, or (b) 15 minutes on a call and I'll tell you what I'd do first. Either way, reply "A" or "B" and I'll stop following up.

Then stop. Move the lead to "lost" and re-touch in 60 days (their situation, not your quota, will have changed).

**Rules that make follow-ups work:** same thread, same channel, no "just checking in", never send the third message on a different platform, and never follow up more than twice in a fortnight.
